Our first walk in Seseh. We meet at the beach, walk through the rice fields to Cemagi Beach hunting for colours, jump in the ocean, then end at the breakfast bar together.

Mosey Club is a walking club for people who'd rather walk and talk than sit and scroll. No pace, no dress code. Come alone, it's even better, or bring your dog.

The plan

Sunday 22 Nov, 6.30am. Meet at Seseh Beach parking lot.
Seseh Beach, through the rice fields (subak), to Cemagi Beach: 8,000 to 10,000 steps.
On the way, a colour hunt game, so bring your phone or a camera. Then a morning ocean dip and the breakfast bar.

Good to know

Free for Vol 1 only (normally Rp 100k), with coffee or a drink, a light breakfast, a host and the colour hunt.
Bring water, swimwear and a towel.
Dogs welcome, under your own care.
Walk at your own pace and your own risk.
We'll confirm your spot on WhatsApp the night before. When it's full, you go on the list for the next walk.

How Dibs works
