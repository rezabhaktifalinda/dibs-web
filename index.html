About Dibs

Dibs are a limited number of reserved spots at a place we've picked, usually a set menu or a table credit, paid upfront. When they're gone, they're gone.

We cover tables, stays, wellness, art and culture: whatever is worth your time. Features are stories about the people behind the places, free to read. Side Quests are small trips off the tourist trail.

Our picks are never for sale. Partnerships exist, and they are always labelled "In partnership with Dibs".

Getting a seat

Every Tuesday, Thursday and Friday at 20:20, something drops: Dibs (reserved spots), a Feature or a Side Quest. List members hear first.

Pay when Dibs go live, by card, Apple Pay, QRIS or transfer. Your QR voucher arrives by email. Book with the venue, then show the QR when you arrive.

You can buy up to 3 per drop and use them together at one table, on one visit.

After you buy

Vouchers are valid for six months from purchase. Event drops use the event date.

Can't make it? Cancel with the venue at least 24 hours before. Later than that, or if you don't turn up, the voucher counts as used.

If the place can't honour your voucher, or an event is cancelled, you get a full refund. Event tickets aren't refunded if you change your mind.
