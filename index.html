Good to know

The spots

A limited number of reserved spots. Valid for 6 months.

The limit

Up to 3 Dibs per person, per drop.

The commitment

Cancel less than 24 hours before, or don't show up, and your spot counts as used.

What we cover

Tables, stays, wellness, art and culture: whatever is worth your time. Features are stories about the people behind the places, free to read. Side Quests are small trips off the tourist trail.

Our picks are never for sale. Partnerships exist, and they are always labelled "In partnership with Dibs".

The fine print

Dibs can be used individually or combined, up to 3 at one table on one visit. Every place has its own rules, so check the details on each drop before you buy. Some releases list closed dates, and event drops use the event date.

If the place can't honour your voucher, or an event is cancelled, you get a full refund. Event tickets aren't refunded if you change your mind.
